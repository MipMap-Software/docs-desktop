## Dual-Screen Comparison

![](/img/en-img/compare.png)

- When multiple-phase tasks exist in the project, click ![](/img/en-img/compare1.png) to enter dual-screen comparison mode on the map.
- Click task thumbnails at the top to select two phases of data for comparison.
- The left-side layer panel displays layers for both phases. You can enable / disable respective output layers and switch between 2D / 3D outputs for comparison.
- Drag the divider in the middle of the map to adjust the screen proportion for outputs of the two tasks.

- Click ![](/img/en-img/exitcompare.png) to exit dual-screen comparison mode.
