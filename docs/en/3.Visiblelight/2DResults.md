## 2D Products

Click the icon ![](/img/cn-img/image92.png) to enable DSM and DOM export for 2D products.

![](/img/en-img/2d.png)

Click ![](/img/en-img/more.png) to perform custom operations on the products.

- Generate Pyramid: When enabled, image pyramids will be generated for fast rendering of images at different scales.

- Tiled Export: When enabled, enter the maximum side length (in pixels). The DOM will be exported in tiles according to this maximum side-length value.

- Resolution: Defaults to Auto. Click Custom GSD to enter a specified resolution. If an excessively small GSD is set, the actual products GSD will be clamped to a fixed value.

> [!warning] Notes
> - 2D products cannot be enabled if imported photos contain no geolocation information.
> - If imported photos are not captured in nadir view, both 2D and 3D products must be enabled. DSM and DOM are generated by nadir projection from the 3D mesh model.
