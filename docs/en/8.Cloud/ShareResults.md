## Share Results


>[!tip] Three sharing modes are available: LAN sharing, Internet sharing and SuperSplat sharing, to support collaborative work, result delivery and external presentation for various scenarios.

![](/img/en-img/share.png)

---

### LAN Sharing

Click to enable LAN Sharing access, select the target local network, and only users within the same LAN can access the model via the link.

![](/img/en-img/share2.png)

- **Select local network address** : Click the dropdown box to select the target LAN.

- **Share Link** : Click the icon to copy the share link or share content.

- **Generate share image** : Click Generate to create a share image. Scan the QR code in the image to view the model. You can download the share image.

> [!warning] Once enabled, this sharing function cannot be manually disabled and will stop automatically when the software is closed.

![](/img/en-img/generate.png)

---

### Internet Sharing

Click to enable Internet Sharing access. Anyone with the link can view the model.

> [!warning] Please read the Terms of Service before sharing, abide by relevant laws and regulations, and refrain from any illegal activities.

![](/img/en-img/share3.png)

- **Share Title** : Customize the display name of your shared model.
- **Description** : Enter a brief description for the shared model.
- **Access Password** : Toggle to enable a 6-character access password. Visitors must enter the correct password to view the model.
**Access Expiration** : Select the validity period for this share link (7 / 14 / 30 / 365 days). The link will expire automatically once the time is up.
- **Share Link** : Click the icon to copy the share link or share content. Click Embed to get the embed code, which can be used to embed the model view into web pages, business systems or third-party platforms.
- **Generate share image** : Click Generate to create a share image. Scan the QR code in the image to view the model. You can download the share image.

![](/img/en-img/generate2.png)

---

### SuperSplat Sharing

Click to enable SuperSplat Sharing access.Upload Gaussian model results to SuperSplat, and a share link will be generated automatically.

> [!tip]Account linking is required the first time.

1. Click **Connect Account** to open the linking window.

![](/img/en-img/connectsupersplat.png)

2. Click **Generate Access Token** to go to the **API Tokens** page in PlayCanvas.

3. Click **GENERATE TOKEN** to create a token, and configure the permissions related to **storage publishing** as instructed by the application.

![](/img/en-img/playcanvas.png)

4. Copy the token, return to MipMap Desktop, and paste it into the **Enter Access Token** field.

5. Click **Verify and Connect** to complete the connection.

6. Enter the result title and description, then click **Upload**.

![](/img/en-img/supersplatshare.png)

7. After a successful upload, the panel will display a **SHARE LINK**.
Click the icon to copy the share link or share content.

![](/img/en-img/supersplatshare2.png)

8. Click **Open in SuperSplat** to navigate to the SuperSplat page.

![](/img/en-img/supersplatshare3.png)

> [!tip]Uploads are Unlisted by default. To make a result publicly visible, change its visibility to Public in SuperSplat and save the setting.
