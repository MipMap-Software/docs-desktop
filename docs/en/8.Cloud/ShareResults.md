## Share Results


>[!tip] For results uploaded to cloud storage, click Share Results. You may select Local-Area-Network sharing or Internet sharing.

![](/img/en-img/share.png)

---

### LAN Sharing

- Click Enable LAN Access and select the connected local-area network. Only users within the current LAN can open and access the link.

- You can edit the share title and enter content description.

- Save the share image; users can scan the QR code in the image to view the model.

- Copy the share link to view the model via web link.

- Once enabled, sharing cannot be turned off manually; it will automatically stop when the app is closed.

![](/img/en-img/share2.png)

---

### Internet Sharing

> [!warning] Please read the Terms of Service before sharing, abide by relevant laws and regulations, and refrain from any illegal activities.

- Click Enable Internet Access. The link will be accessible to all users.
- You can edit the share title and enter content description.
- Enable Access Password. A six-digit password can be randomly generated or manually set. Other users must enter the correct password to gain access.
- Access validity period defaults to 7 days. Click to modify the validity period.
- Save the share image; users can scan the QR code in the image to view the model.
- Copy the share link to view the model via web link.
- Sharing becomes invalid when you disable Internet Access on this panel or when the access validity period expires.

![](/img/en-img/share3.png)
