## Line Measurement

![](/img/en-img/line.png)

---

### Operation Steps

- Click Line Measurement to draw a polyline on outputs for measurement.
- Left-click to add vertices, right-click to undo the last vertex, and double-click the left mouse button to finish polyline drawing and complete measurement. Press ESC to exit measurement mode.
- Measurement information of the polyline is displayed on the left panel: Planar Length, Spatial Length, Minimum Slope, Maximum Slope, Minimum Height, Maximum Height, and Height Difference.
- You can modify the name and color of the line annotation.

---

### Measurement Definitions
- Planar Length: Total length of the polyline projected onto the XY horizontal plane.
- Spatial Length: Total length of the polyline in XYZ 3D space.
- Minimum / Maximum Slope: The minimum / maximum slope value along the polyline.
- Minimum / Maximum Height: The minimum / maximum Height value along the polyline.
- Height Difference = Maximum Height − Minimum Height.
