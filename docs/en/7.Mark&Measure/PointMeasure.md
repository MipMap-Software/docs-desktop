## Point Measurement

![](/img/en-img/point.png)

---

### Operation Steps

- Click Point Measurement. Left-click the target point on outputs to complete the measurement. Press ESC to exit measurement mode.
- Coordinate information of the point is displayed on the left panel: Longitude, Latitude, Elevation, Normal Height (elevation coordinate system must be selected).
- Click View Cameras to show all cameras that captured this point (Dataset-Image layer must be enabled).
- You can modify the name and color of the point annotation.
