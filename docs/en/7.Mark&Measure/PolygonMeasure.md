## Polygon Measurement

![](/img/en-img/polygon2.png)

---

### Operation Steps
- Click Polygon Measurement to draw a polygon on outputs for measurement.
- Left-click to add vertices, right-click to undo the last vertex, and double-click the left mouse button to finish polygon drawing and complete measurement. Press ESC to exit measurement mode.
- Measurement information of the polygon is displayed on the left panel: Planar Polygon, Spatial Polygon, Planar Perimeter, Spatial Perimeter, Minimum Height, Maximum Height, and Height Difference.
- You can modify the name and color of the polygon annotation.

---

### Measurement Definitions
- Planar Polygon: Polygon of the measured polygon projected onto the XY plane.
- Spatial Polygon: Polygon of the measured polygon in XYZ 3D space.
- Planar Perimeter: Perimeter of the measured polygon projected onto the XY plane.
- Spatial Perimeter: Perimeter of the measured polygon in XYZ 3D space.
- Minimum / Maximum Height: The minimum / maximum Height value within the measured polygon.
- Height Difference = Maximum Height − Minimum Height.
