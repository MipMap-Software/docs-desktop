## 3D Products

Click the icon ![](/img/cn-img/image92.png) to enable 3D Products.

![](/img/en-img/3d.png)

---

### Textured Model

![](/img/en-img/model2.png)

- When Textured Model export is enabled, B3DM and OSGB formats are exported by default.
- Click the dropdown box to select desired products formats.

---

### Point Cloud

![](/img/en-img/pointcloud2.png)

- When point cloud export is enabled, PNTS and LAS formats are exported by default.
- Click the dropdown box to select desired products formats.

---

### Gaussian Splatting

![](/img/en-img/gs2.png)

- When Gaussian Splatting is enabled, SOGTiles, PLY and SOG formats are exported by default.
- Max Gaussian Points: Defaults to Auto. You can customize the Gaussian point count to control the size of Gaussian products.
- People Removal: When enabled, the influence of people in frames on Gaussian products can be eliminated.
- Click the dropdown box to select desired products formats.
> [!tip] A Gaussian PLY with one million points is approximately 90 MB. Adjust the Gaussian point count as needed.
  
---

### Products Coordinate System

Select the coordinate system and vertical datum for 3D products; you may search by keyword. For a custom coordinate system for 3D products, import a PRJ file.
