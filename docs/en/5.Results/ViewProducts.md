## View Products

![](/img/en-img/viewproducts.png)

Click View Products to open the folder containing result files.

![](/img/en-img/result.png)

---

### 2D Products

The 2D folder stores all 2D products.

![](/img/en-img/result2d.png)
-   dom_tiles: Tiled orthoimagery products.
-   dsm_tiles: Tiled Digital Surface Model products.
-   geotiffs: Orthoimagery (DOM), Digital Surface Model (DSM) and other products.
-   split_dom: Tiled-sheet orthoimagery products (Tiled export must be enabled).

---

### 3D Products

The 3D folder stores all 3D products.

![](/img/en-img/result3d.png)

#### Mesh Model products
-   model-b3dm: 3D model products in 3DTiles format.
-   model-fbx: 3D model products in FBX format.
-   model-glb: 3D model products in GLB format.
-   model-obj: 3D model products in OBJ format.
-   model-osgb: 3D model products in OSGB format.
-   model-ply: 3D model products in PLY format.
-   model-skp: 3D model products in SKP format.

#### Gaussian-Splatting products
-   model-gs-ply: Gaussian-Splatting products in PLY format.
-   model-gs-sog: Gaussian-Splatting products in SOG format.
-   model-gs-sog-tile: Gaussian-Splatting products in SOG Tile format.
-   model-gs-splat-tile: Gaussian-Splatting products in Splat Tile format.

#### Point-Cloud products
-   point-las: Point-cloud products in LAS format.
-   point-osgb: Point-cloud products in OSGB format.
-   point-ply: Point-cloud products in PLY format.
-   point-pnts: Point-cloud products in PNTS format.

---

### Aerial-Triangulation Products

The AT folder stores aerial-triangulation output files. `mvs` contains raw aerial-triangulation products; `mvs_undistort` contains undistorted aerial-triangulation products.

![](/img/en-img/resultat.png)

---

### Log Files

The logs folder stores project log files.

>[!tip] If reconstruction fails, send the log files to technical support for error diagnosis.

![](/img/en-img/logs.png)

---

### Thumbnails

The thumbnail folder stores project thumbnails.

![](/img/en-img/thumbnail.png)

-   camera_1_residual: Camera residual distribution map.
-   overlap_map: Overlap-coverage distribution map.
-   rgb_thumbnail: Stores snapshots captured via ![](/img/en-img/saveview.png), standard-resolution thumbnail.

-   rgb_thumbnail_4K: 4K high-resolution DOM thumbnail.
