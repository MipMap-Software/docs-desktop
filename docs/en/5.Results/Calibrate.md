### Scale

Define the real-world size of the model. Enter the actual length between the two selected endpoints, and the system will automatically scale the model to the real dimension.

>[!tip] Steps:
>1. Click the Scale icon
>2. Left-click two endpoints in sequence on the 3D/GS model
>3. Enter the real length of this segment
>4. Click Confirm and wait for the model to finish updating

![](/img/en-img/scale.png)

---

### Groundplane

Set the reference ground plane of the model to correct tilt and level the model.

>[!tip] Steps:
>1. Click the Groundplane icon
>2. Left-click three points on the ground area of the 3D/GS model to define the ground reference plane. The blue arrow indicates the upward vertical direction of the leveled model.
>3. Click Confirm and wait for the model to finish updating

![](/img/en-img/groundplane.png)
